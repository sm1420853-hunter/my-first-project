WHO WE ARE

We don't manage poverty from an office. We share tools under the shade.

Traditional development projects are often designed in distant cities by people who have never walked our streets. **Digital And Financial Literacy** operates entirely on the ground. We live here, we work here, and we understand our local market rhythm.

Walk through Kakuma and you will see incredible energy—women frying golden mandazis, youth repairing electronics, and colorful mango stalls. We don’t change what our community sells; we upgrade *how* they grow profits by bringing ledger training directly to them.

Our Core Mission

To empower youth dropouts and women micro-merchants through practical math, secure mobile-wallet setups, and community table-banking safety nets that break dependency cycles.
